Effect benchmark

Every built-in effect, drawn into a 1920×1080 world buffer over a traced facade — the same renderer and the same geometry a projector tab uses. Each effect is measured twice, pointed at five windows and again covering the whole frame, and the worse of the two is reported, because that is the cost you can actually hit.

A projector has 16.7 ms a frame for everything, and the bloom and warp want a few of those. A show is a dozen layers, so roughly 1 ms per effect is the budget; anything over 4 ms will cost frames on its own.
